## Key question 1 — Teams: creating teams, team settings page, team types (rehearsal / secure / split / tag), service-type assignment, related teams, scheduling defaults, "Replies to", gap alerts, rescheduling declines, options (last scheduled, scheduled-viewer access, signup sheets), leaders, archiving/deleting/restoring/merging, automations

### Takeaway
A team is now an account-level object (since Feb 2026) that can be assigned to zero, one or many service types; its "Settings" tab holds type checkboxes ("Rehearsal Team", "Secure Team", "Split Team", "Tag Team"), "Service Types", "Related teams", "Scheduling defaults" ("Status", "Notification"/"Prepared", "Replies to"), "Scheduling gap alerts", "Rescheduling declines" (5 options incl. "Volunteer Replacement" and "Auto-schedule"), and "Options" ("Last Scheduled Date", "Access for Scheduled Viewers", "Signup Sheets"). Leaders are managed on the "Members" tab > "Team leaders" with per-leader "Accept"/"Decline"/"Blockouts" notification checkboxes; teams can be archived (keeps history), deleted (30-day recovery) or merged.

**Team settings page — "Type" section**
- "Check the boxes to set up your team as one or more of the following types":

- Split team details: "If the teams for each service time have different leaders, create two separate teams instead"; recommended order: set service type's time preferences → create split team → assign time preferences → schedule. Convert an existing team: Settings tab > "Check the box under the Split Team section." Merging: "Merging teams cannot be undone. The old teams will be deleted and can only be recovered for up to 30 days after the merge. The old teams' scheduling history will be tied to the newly merged team and cannot be reverted". Scheduling a split team: plan > "Teams" tab > "Team actions" > "Needed Positions" — "Split team positions will appear for each service time on this plan"; use ➕/➖ per service time; "Done". On a template the positions appear "for each time preference listed in this service type's settings". Reassigning a scheduled split-team member: select their name > uncheck service-time boxes > "Save"; "If you remove a person from the only time they were assigned to on a split team, they are completely removed from the plan." — [Set up split teams](https://help.planningcenter.com/en/142899-set-up-split-teams.html)
- Tag team details: Settings > "Check the box under the Tag Team section"; popup "Yes, clear members" (new tag team: all previously assigned members removed) or "Yes, change members" (previously a tag team); "a tag icon shows next to its name"; unchecking the box keeps members. "When the tag Team type is enabled, the Add Person button changes to Add tag." Positions get tags: team "Members" tab > position > "Add tag" > search > select; "Only one tag per group can be selected. The resulting list of people must be assigned to all tags you add to each position"; "It is not recommended to add the same tag to multiple positions on the same team." "People tagged with a position tag will automatically be added to the team when the tag is added to the position. If you remove the tag from the person, they will be removed from the team." "If a tag team exists with the same name as a people tag group, then any new tag added to that tag group will be created as a position within the tag team." — [Set up tag teams](https://help.planningcenter.com/en/142901-set-up-tag-teams.html); [Should my team be a tag team?](https://help.planningcenter.com/en/142902-should-my-team-be-a-tag-team-.html); [Organize people with tags](https://help.planningcenter.com/en/142861-organize-people-with-tags.html)

### Inferences
- The `viewers_see` integer on `Team` almost certainly encodes the "Access for Scheduled Viewers" option ("Full Plan" vs "My Schedule Only"); `last_plan_from` encodes the "Last Scheduled Date" option; `default_status` = "Unconfirmed"/"Confirmed"; `default_prepare_notifications` = the "Prepared" checkbox; `stage_color`/`stage_variant` likely relate to the Stage/LIVE display colour (out of scope).
- The "Rescheduling declines" option set appears to have grown from 4 to 5 values with the May 2026 "Volunteer Replacement" release; `Schedule.reschedule_declines` in the API exposes the team's chosen value to the volunteer-side client.
- Because "Adding someone as a team leader grants them scheduler permissions in that service type", leader status is effectively a permission grant scoped to the team's service types.

## Key question 2 — Positions: team positions, order, "Needed positions" per plan/time, position tags, position-level time assignment, attachment types

### Takeaway
Positions are created and reordered (six-dot drag) on the team's "Members" tab; "Needed positions" are per-plan (or per-template) counts set with ➕/➖ in a "Needed Positions" editor (per service time for split teams); since April 2025 individual positions can be assigned to plan times, and tag-team positions are filled by tag combinations.

### Cited Findings
- Add positions: team page > "Add position" button below the left sidebar > enter name > "Add". "Position names should not include times (e.g., 'Greeter 9AM'). Set up split teams to assign people to specific service times." "Reorder positions by dragging the six-dot icon next to a position's name". Edit: select position > ✏️ edit icon next to name > "Save". Delete: ⚙️ settings icon > "Delete position" > "Yes, delete" ("no way to recover that position or its previously assigned team members"). Remove a member: hover name > red ⊖ > "Yes, remove". — [Create or edit a team](https://help.planningcenter.com/en/140130-create-or-edit-a-team.html)
- Add team members to a position: select position > "Add person" (top right) > search name; "You can also search for a tag or another position to assign people from that tag or position"; "Create a new person: [Name]" if not in People (team leaders can only create profiles if default permission settings allow schedulers to "edit contact info and households" and "view people not on My teams"). "You can add people to more than one position on a team, but if you have a large team you want to schedule all at once, it is recommended that each team member be added to only one position." ## Key question 5 — Blockouts and conflicts: how volunteers set blockouts (web / Services Mobile / Church Center / email link), fields (all-day, times, repeat, reason, household, linked accounts), editing one/future/all, admin visibility, conflict types and badges, how conflicts are computed

## Key question 8 — Communication: scheduling emails (content, recipients popup, templates), reminder emails (timing, content, plan notes), gap alerts, email templates (types, placeholders, personal/shared), texting (Services Mobile group text; SMS-gateway text notifications), push notifications & preferred app, Chat (teams/plans/DMs), plan notes, communication history, undelivered emails

### Takeaway
Scheduling emails are sent from a plan ("Send" per team, ✉️ per person while prepared, ✉️ for the whole plan) or from the matrix, through a "Recipients" popup (Teams / Times / Include) then an editor with template dropdown, attachments (≤10 MB each, 5 per email) and inline images; they contain assigned times, led items, custom text, "Accept"/"Decline" buttons, and "View plan". Reminders go out at 10 a.m. local time 0–7 days before each plan time (per team per time) and include assigned plan notes. Texting = device group text from Services Mobile (with plan short link, "Include phone reference") or carrier SMS-gateway notifications with accept/decline short links; Chat (since May 2024) offers team, plan-filtered and direct-message conversations.
